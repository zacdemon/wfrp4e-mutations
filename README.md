# wfrp4e-mutations
WFRP4E module for Foundry based on the Mutation's Handbook by Anders Hellspong

## Unreleased: handbook mutation flow
- New world setting **Use Mutant's Handbook rules** (on by default). A failed Dissolution of Body and Mind test (and `/table corruption`, `mutatephys`, `mutatemental` or their `@Table` links) runs the handbook flow instead of the system's: species type roll (p8), Corruption loss of TB or WPB (p8), severity roll with +10 per mutation up to +40 (p9), source choice, the module's compendium tables (no world import needed) with "roll on the next table" and sub-tables followed automatically, and a result card where the player (on an actor they own) or the GM adds the mutation with *Add Mutation*, the player spends 1 Resilience to avoid it (p9), or the GM discards it (players clicking *Discard* get a no-permission message). Chosen of Chaos lets the GM pick the patron (p74). A Chaos Spawn warning goes to the GM when the limits on p10 are passed. Turn the setting off for stock system behaviour.
- Optional rules as settings, off by default: **Keeping it Random** and **Let them off with a warning!** (p9). Also: ask for the mutation source, and the species used for species the handbook table doesn't list.
- The Generate Mutation macro is now a DialogV2 dialog that runs the same flow. The old world-script entry point `game.wfrp4eMutationRoll(actor, source)` still works.
- Uses libWrapper when it is active; otherwise patches the system directly.
- **Granted items** (world setting *Add the Talents, Traits, items and rolled modifiers a mutation grants*, on by default). 127 mutations carry an effect that adds what their text grants when the mutation lands on an actor: wfrp4e-core Talents (one more level each time, p10), Creature Traits with their damage or rating (the specification, plus SB/TB where the core Trait adds it; Ensnaring Tongue's Tongue Attack does SB damage), Psychology with a prompted target, Skill advances (Oracle's Augury and Scrying Touch's Psychometry come from Winds of Magic, a recommended module; without it they are Advanced Int placeholder skills with a GM note), natural armour on the right hit locations, and linked mutations (Bestial Arms (Bat) adds Protective Skin (Leathery Hide) and Razor-sharp Claws, and so on). The in-text rolls (Fangs, Long Spines, Possessed, Malign Sorcerer...) are rolled, choices ("X or Y", "pick one", "pick two") are prompted, and sub-table results the flow recorded (Protective Skin, Wings, Tail, Corrupted Blood, Immunity, Bestial/Unnatural Body) are used; without one you can roll or pick. Repeats follow the book (Horns, Fangs and Corrosive Vomit +1 Damage; Irrational Contempt goes Prejudice, Animosity, Hatred). A GM-only card lists what was rolled and added. Deleting the mutation removes everything it added and undoes skill advances and raised ratings. The specs live in `tools/grants-data.mjs`; `node tools/grants.mjs` writes them into the item sources.
- **Characteristic, Movement, Wounds and test modifiers**, using the system's own effect machinery. Each mutation with modifiers carries a *<Mutation> (Modifiers)* effect: Characteristic and Movement changes are ActiveEffect changes (`system.characteristics.X.modifier`, `system.details.move.value`), Wounds are added by a `woundCalc` script after the size formula, and situational test modifiers are roll-dialog options (pre-ticked where they always apply, such as Bizarre Colouration's -10 Stealth; offered where they depend on the scene, such as Midnight Skin's +20 Stealth in darkness). Rolled values (Brain Rot -1d10 Intelligence, Twisted Body -2d10 Agility...) are rolled once when the mutation is added and stored as plain numbers on the effect; hit-location results (Itching Rash, Running Sores, Unhealing Wound: -10 Fellowship on the head), Bestial/Unnatural body parts, Monstrous Growth / Pitiful Stature size steps, and "X or Y" picks are applied the same way. "While X" modifiers (Lunacy's Morrslieb phases, Painseeker, Oracle, -10 Fellowship for the rest of the scene) are separate effects on the item, switched off until you need them. Each copy has its own effect, so repeats stack (p10); Characteristics never drop below 0 and Wounds never below 1. Deleting the mutation removes its effects. Fixed values live in `tools/modifiers-data.mjs` (with the list of what stays by hand); `npm run effects` rewrites both effect sets.
- **Fortune, mutation powers, rolls made against the mutant, and Criticals.** Fortune, Fate, Resilience and Resolve are pools the sheet spends, so they are changed by one-off updates (with a chat line), never by ActiveEffect changes. Time Warp grants the wfrp4e-core Luck Talent exactly as core does it (core's own Luck grants, e.g. the Luck weapon quality, just create a Luck item): Luck has no script, it raises *maximum* Fortune (Fate + Luck levels), which the session Fortune reset macros (GM Toolkit's counts Luck) apply; current Fortune is not touched. Deleting Time Warp removes that Luck level. Buttons on the mutation's effects (actor's Effects tab): Power Item Obsession *Power item not on me: -1 Fortune* / *Power item back: +1 Fortune*; Oracle *Activate Oracle* rolls the Challenging (+0) Willpower Test (a fumble rolls a Minor Miscast) and on a success switches on +10 Initiative and adds +1 Fortune per level, and *End Oracle* at the end of the scene removes what is left of that Fortune; Lunacy *Activate Lunacy* asks for Morrslieb's state (daytime, absent, small, large, full) and switches on that phase's effect, full Morrslieb adding +1 Fortune (unspent, it goes again when the moon changes). Long arms (Elongated Limbs) make the mutant's weapon one Reach step longer in the system's Weapon Length check: an attacker with the same Reach as a long-armed defender takes the -10 for the shorter weapon, and a long-armed attacker facing a weapon one step longer takes none. As in the system (wfrp4e 10.1.2 checkPostModifiers) only the Infighter Talent skips the comparison; the In-fighting status works through the Improvised weapons it gives. Modifiers to other people's rolls use the system's targeter dialog scripts: when someone targets the mutant, the option appears in *their* roll dialog: Alien Mind -30 to Intuition (pre-ticked) and to Willpower/casting attempts to read the mind (offered), Skinwalker -20 to Perception/Intuition for observers with Second Sight (offered), long arms +20 to melee attacks, pre-ticked when the attacker is in-fighting. Savagery adds +10 to the Critical link of its attacks (and to wound Criticals under Up in Arms Criticals); a long neck adds +20 to head wound Criticals under Up in Arms Criticals. Wound Criticals: wfrp4e 10.1.2 builds the Critical link after the damage scripts and reads no modifier from them, so the module adds these when the link on the opposed-test card is clicked (a `wfrp4e:rollTable` hook; also works with the Expanded Criticals chart picker). Also: Hideous Appearance caps Fellowship at 0, Regeneration has a *Critical Wound regenerated: -1 Fellowship* button, Shapeshifter offers its -10-per-Wound-lost Endurance option.
