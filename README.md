# wfrp4e-mutations
WFRP4E module for Foundry based on the Mutation's Handbook by Anders Hellspong

## Unreleased: handbook mutation flow
- New world setting **Use Mutant's Handbook rules** (on by default). A failed Dissolution of Body and Mind test (and `/table corruption`, `mutatephys`, `mutatemental` or their `@Table` links) runs the handbook flow instead of the system's: species type roll (p8), Corruption loss of TB or WPB (p8), severity roll with +10 per mutation up to +40 (p9), source choice, the module's compendium tables (no world import needed) with "roll on the next table" and sub-tables followed automatically, and a result card where the player (on an actor they own) or the GM adds the mutation with *Add Mutation*, the player spends 1 Resilience to avoid it (p9), or the GM discards it (players clicking *Discard* get a no-permission message). Chosen of Chaos lets the GM pick the patron (p74). A Chaos Spawn warning goes to the GM when the limits on p10 are passed. Turn the setting off for stock system behaviour.
- **Fortune and rerolls on the Dissolution test.** The flow is linked to the test that started it (a flow id saved in the test message's data, and on every card the flow posts, with the Corruption it removed, the mutation it added, any Resilience spent and any patron set). If the player rerolls the test with Fortune (or the GM rerolls it, or a Dark Deal does) or adds +1 SL, and the test now passes, the module undoes the flow: the added mutation is deleted (its own revert removes everything it granted), the exact Corruption lost is put back (without starting another Dissolution test), Resilience spent on *Spend 1 Resilience to avoid it* is refunded, a patron set from the card is removed, and the card is marked *Avoided with Fortune* (*Avoided on a reroll* for a non-Fortune reroll) with its buttons gone. If the test still fails, the card gets a note and nothing runs a second time. A reroll made while the flow's source dialog is still open closes the dialog and posts the card as avoided. A player who can't edit the card (e.g. the GM rolled the test) has the active GM make the change through the module socket. Note: in wfrp4e 10.1.2, +1 SL never turns a failed roll into a success (success is the roll against the target), so in practice +1 SL only adds the "still failed" note; the undo covers it for house rules or effects that do let it pass. Cards from older builds aren't linked; for those the GM still gets the old whisper to sort it out by hand.
- Optional rules as settings, off by default: **Keeping it Random** and **Let them off with a warning!** (p9). Also: ask for the mutation source, and the species used for species the handbook table doesn't list.
- The Generate Mutation macro is now a DialogV2 dialog that runs the same flow. The old world-script entry point `game.wfrp4eMutationRoll(actor, source)` still works.
- Uses libWrapper when it is active; otherwise patches the system directly.
- **Granted items** (world setting *Add the Talents, Traits, items and rolled modifiers a mutation grants*, on by default). 127 mutations carry an effect that adds what their text grants when the mutation lands on an actor: wfrp4e-core Talents (one more level each time, p10), Creature Traits with their damage or rating (the specification, plus SB/TB where the core Trait adds it; Ensnaring Tongue's Tongue Attack does SB damage), Psychology with a prompted target, Skill advances (Oracle's Augury and Scrying Touch's Psychometry come from Winds of Magic, a recommended module; without it they are Advanced Int placeholder skills with a GM note), natural armour on the right hit locations, and linked mutations (Bestial Arms (Bat) adds Protective Skin (Leathery Hide) and Razor-sharp Claws, and so on). The in-text rolls (Fangs, Long Spines, Possessed, Malign Sorcerer...) are rolled, choices ("X or Y", "pick one", "pick two") are prompted, and sub-table results the flow recorded (Protective Skin, Wings, Tail, Corrupted Blood, Immunity, Bestial/Unnatural Body) are used; without one you can roll or pick. Repeats follow the book (Horns, Fangs and Corrosive Vomit +1 Damage; Irrational Contempt goes Prejudice, Animosity, Hatred). A GM-only card lists what was rolled and added. Deleting the mutation removes everything it added and undoes skill advances and raised ratings. The specs live in `tools/grants-data.mjs`; `node tools/grants.mjs` writes them into the item sources.
- **Characteristic, Movement, Wounds and test modifiers**, using the system's own effect machinery. Each mutation with modifiers carries a *<Mutation> (Modifiers)* effect: Characteristic and Movement changes are ActiveEffect changes (`system.characteristics.X.modifier`, `system.details.move.value`), Wounds are added by a `woundCalc` script after the size formula, and situational test modifiers are roll-dialog options (pre-ticked where they always apply, such as Bizarre Colouration's -10 Stealth; offered where they depend on the scene, such as Midnight Skin's +20 Stealth in darkness). Rolled values (Brain Rot -1d10 Intelligence, Twisted Body -2d10 Agility...) are rolled once when the mutation is added and stored as plain numbers on the effect; hit-location results (Itching Rash, Running Sores, Unhealing Wound: -10 Fellowship on the head), Bestial/Unnatural body parts, Monstrous Growth / Pitiful Stature size steps, and "X or Y" picks are applied the same way. "While X" modifiers (Lunacy's Morrslieb phases, Painseeker, Oracle, -10 Fellowship for the rest of the scene) are separate effects on the item, switched off until you need them. Each copy has its own effect, so repeats stack (p10); Characteristics never drop below 0 and Wounds never below 1. Deleting the mutation removes its effects. Fixed values live in `tools/modifiers-data.mjs` (with the list of what stays by hand); `npm run effects` rewrites both effect sets.
- **Fortune, mutation powers, rolls made against the mutant, and Criticals.** Fortune, Fate, Resilience and Resolve are pools the sheet spends, so they are changed by one-off updates (with a chat line), never by ActiveEffect changes. Time Warp grants the wfrp4e-core Luck Talent exactly as core does it (core's own Luck grants, e.g. the Luck weapon quality, just create a Luck item): Luck has no script, it raises *maximum* Fortune (Fate + Luck levels), which the session Fortune reset macros (GM Toolkit's counts Luck) apply; current Fortune is not touched. Deleting Time Warp removes that Luck level. Buttons on the mutation's effects (actor's Effects tab): Power Item Obsession *Power item not on me: -1 Fortune* / *Power item back: +1 Fortune*; Oracle *Activate Oracle* rolls the Challenging (+0) Willpower Test (a fumble rolls a Minor Miscast) and on a success switches on +10 Initiative and adds +1 Fortune per level, and *End Oracle* at the end of the scene removes what is left of that Fortune; Lunacy *Activate Lunacy* asks for Morrslieb's state (daytime, absent, small, large, full) and switches on that phase's effect, full Morrslieb adding +1 Fortune (unspent, it goes again when the moon changes). Long arms (Elongated Limbs) make the mutant's weapon one Reach step longer in the system's Weapon Length check: an attacker with the same Reach as a long-armed defender takes the -10 for the shorter weapon, and a long-armed attacker facing a weapon one step longer takes none. As in the system (wfrp4e 10.1.2 checkPostModifiers) only the Infighter Talent skips the comparison; the In-fighting status works through the Improvised weapons it gives. Modifiers to other people's rolls use the system's targeter dialog scripts: when someone targets the mutant, the option appears in *their* roll dialog: Alien Mind -30 to Intuition (pre-ticked) and to Willpower/casting attempts to read the mind (offered), Skinwalker -20 to Perception/Intuition for observers with Second Sight (offered), long arms +20 to melee attacks, pre-ticked when the attacker is in-fighting. Savagery adds +10 to the Critical link of its attacks (and to wound Criticals under Up in Arms Criticals); a long neck adds +20 to head wound Criticals under Up in Arms Criticals. Wound Criticals: wfrp4e 10.1.2 builds the Critical link after the damage scripts and reads no modifier from them, so the module adds these when the link on the opposed-test card is clicked (a `wfrp4e:rollTable` hook; also works with the Expanded Criticals chart picker). Also: Hideous Appearance caps Fellowship at 0, Regeneration has a *Critical Wound regenerated: -1 Fellowship* button, Shapeshifter offers its -10-per-Wound-lost Endurance option.
- **Test-activated powers** (`scripts/mutation-activations.mjs`). 31 mutations have a button on their *(Modifiers)* effect (actor's Effects tab, first copy only) that rolls the handbook's Test at its printed Difficulty and applies the result. A Fumble rolls the Miscast the text names (Minor; Major for Temporal Instability). The outcome posts a card with the numbers worked out from the Test and a *GM:* line for what the module can't judge.
  - **Timed powers** create an effect on the actor with the book's duration. Rounds count down on the combat tracker and the effect expires like any system effect; minutes and hours follow world time. Each has an *End <Mutation>* button. Traits come from the switched-off copies the mutation granted, which are switched on while the effect lasts and off again when it ends; without one, a wfrp4e-core copy is added and deleted with the effect. *Activate Ethereal* (Challenging (+0) Willpower, Ethereal Trait for Willpower Bonus + SL Rounds), *Activate Invisibility* (Challenging Willpower, Willpower + SL Rounds, doubled per extra copy), *Activate Levitation* (Challenging Willpower, Flight 30 x levels for Willpower Bonus + SL Rounds, doubled per extra copy), *Activate Swarmform* (Challenging Endurance, Swarm Trait until ended; the swarm's creatures are rolled once and kept), *Activate Shapeshifter* (Challenging Endurance, Toughness Bonus + SL hours), *Activate Werebeast* (asks turn bestial or change back and Morrslieb's state, then Challenging Willpower with the Morrslieb modifier; the form lasts until changing back), *Activate Mirror Image* (Challenging Willpower, Willpower + SL x 10 minutes, doubled per extra copy), *Activate Phantasmal Mind* (create: Challenging Willpower, Willpower + SL x 10 minutes; animate: Hard (-20) Willpower), *Activate Telekinesis* (move things: Average (+20) Willpower, Willpower Rounds; hurl: Challenging Willpower, then the BS Test).
  - **Applied on the card's targets.** *Activate Life Leech* (Challenging Willpower): SL (+1 per extra copy) Damage ignoring Armour to the one targeted token, as the system applies it, and the mutant heals the Wounds lost; with no target it drains an object and heals the full amount. *Activate Bloomblight Touch* (Challenging Willpower): heals the targeted creature Fellowship + SL Wounds, rolls the symptom (1d100) and days (1d10), and its owner rolls the Challenging Endurance Test; a failure adds core's symptom effect for those days. *Activate Thunderhead* lightning bolt (Challenging): Willpower Bonus + SL Damage at a rolled hit location where only non-metal armour protects, and 1 Blinded. *Activate Pyrokinesis* blast (Challenging): Willpower Bonus + SL Damage at a rolled location to every targeted token, each rolling an Average (+20) Endurance Test or gaining 1 Ablaze; ignite (Average) sets targeted tokens Ablaze. *Activate Fleshcrafter* (Trade (Fleshcrafter); cosmetic Challenging, drastic Difficult (-10), bone Hard (-20), stop bleeding Challenging): stopping bleeding removes SL Bleeding from the target or yourself. Targets the player doesn't own are changed by the active GM (Foundry query); a follow-up Test goes to the creature's active player, else the GM. *Activate Temporal Instability* rolls the ageing 1d10 and adds a year to a numeric age on a 10. *Activate Dimensional Instability* asks for passengers and worsens Average (+20) one step per passenger and per teleport since the last rest; *Rested 8 hours* resets it.
  - **Card only, with the values worked out:** *Activate Green Sovereign*, *Activate Wind Caller* (small things Average; strike or gust Challenging), *Activate Telepathy* project thoughts (Average). Every Telepathy use is its own Test: nothing lasts or blocks the next try.
  - **Opposed** (*Activate Evil Eye*, *Activate Entrancement*, *Activate Beast Alpha*, Telepathy's pluck thoughts, Thunderhead's touch) roll against the targeted token through the system's opposed Test. Once the defender rolls, the active GM applies the outcome once per defender: Evil Eye gives the target 1 Fatigued and a Hard (-20) Endurance Test (Prone on a failure), or the mutant 1 Stunned on a loss; Thunderhead's touch Stuns; Entrancement adds *Entranced by <mutant>* for Willpower Bonus + opposed SL hours; pluck thoughts says whether the target noticed (under 2 SL); Beast Alpha says whether the beast obeys.
  - **Tests the mutation forces on you:** *Suppress Chameleon Skin (Cool Test)*, *Splitting Headache strikes (Cool Test)* (a failure adds Stunned), *Resist the demon (Willpower Test)* for Possessed (one step harder per extra copy; a failure gives the demon failed-SL seconds, minutes or hours), and a *Morning Cool Test* for Chosen One, False Wizard, Doom Prophet, Metamorphosis, Rebellious Mind and Saint of the Damned, with the book's pass or fail outcome on the card.
  - Not buttons: attacks the system already rolls with a Trait or weapon (Acidic Saliva, Gut Worm, Piercing Tongue, Thorns, Hungering Maw), and Tests other people make against the mutant (Alien Mind, Forgettable Face, Tantalising Aura...). Fortune rerolls of a power Test don't re-run the power. The button list is `POWER_BUTTONS` in `scripts/modifier-scripts.mjs`, and `npm run validate` checks every entry has its button, its POWERS entry and its localisation.
- **Choices the text gives the character are asked when the mutation is added**, of the actor's active player (else the GM) through a Foundry query; closing the dialog or no answer within 5 minutes leaves a note on the GM's summary card, and nothing is guessed. New prompts: *Fright* (the source is the flow's Fright roll, or picked or rolled now; a category can be narrowed to something specific; on a repeat you raise an existing source's Terror or add a new source, and the *Face your fright (Terror Test)* button posts the Terror Test at that source's rating), *Protective Skin* (replace natural armour another mutation already gives those locations, which is unequipped and worn again if Protective Skin is removed, or keep both), and the mutation is renamed with the answer for *False Wizard* (lore), *Chosen One* (god), *Glowing Heart* (hue), *Prince of Nothing* (family and status), *Rebellious Mind* (cause), *Bizarre Colouration* (pattern) and *Insect Eyes* (fly or spider). Choices that were already prompted are unchanged (body-part picks, sub-table pick-or-roll, specialisations, power use pickers). Choice texts that are left to play (Hungering Maw bite or grapple, Crossbreed, cosmetic either/ors...) are listed with a reason in `CHOICE_EXCEPTIONS` in `tools/grants-data.mjs`; `npm run validate` fails on any mutation whose text offers a choice with neither a prompt nor an exception.
